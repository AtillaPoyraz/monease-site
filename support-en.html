Frequently asked questions

How do I delete financial entries?

You can delete transactions within the app. Uninstalling the app may remove local data; copies held in device or iCloud backups are controlled by Apple's backup settings.

How can I manage my Premium subscription?

Open the subscriptions section of your Apple Account to review or cancel a subscription. If Premium access is missing, use “Restore purchases” in Monease.

How do I make a privacy or deletion request?

Send an email. Please do not include passwords, payment card information, or detailed financial records. Purchase records maintained by Apple and RevenueCat have their own retention policies.
