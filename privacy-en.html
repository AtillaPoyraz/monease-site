Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Monease is a personal finance tracker that stores the financial records you enter locally on your device. This policy describes how the current version of Monease handles the relevant information. We update it when these practices change.

1. Who operates Monease

Monease is operated by Atilla Poyraz. Questions about personal data and deletion requests can be sent to atilla.poyraz1998@gmail.com.

2. Financial records stored on your device

Income and expense entries, categories, budgets, expected income, recurring bill plans, cash balance snapshots and related forecasts are stored locally using SwiftData. Monease does not connect to bank accounts or transmit those manual financial entries to RevenueCat. Data may be included in device backups depending on your Apple backup settings.

3. Subscription services

Premium subscription status and purchases are handled through Apple in-app purchases and RevenueCat. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, purchase history, receipts and certain device or transaction metadata to provide subscription entitlements, fraud prevention, and service operation. Review RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

4. How information is used

On-device financial data is used to show your recorded balance, spending summaries, budgets, scheduled bills and cash-flow estimates. Subscription transaction data is processed for purchase verification, entitlement restoration, billing support and service analytics where applicable. Monease does not currently include advertising SDKs or bank-account integrations.

5. Retention and deletion

You can edit or delete individual transactions in the app. Removing the app may delete local app records on that device, but Apple backups may preserve copies according to your settings. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction information for their own operational or legal obligations. For requests concerning personal data handled by the app operator, contact atilla.poyraz1998@gmail.com; for Apple-managed purchases, use Apple subscription and privacy controls.

6. Security and transfers

Monease relies on iOS application storage protections for locally stored data. Subscription processing involves Apple and RevenueCat services and may involve data transfers outside your country under their policies and contracts. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Email support

If you contact us for support, feedback or privacy requests, your email address, message and any attachments you voluntarily share may be processed through Google's email services. We use them to answer your request and comply with applicable obligations. We may retain this correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and any applicable legal requirements. You do not need to send complete financial records, identification documents or passwords to receive help; please avoid sharing sensitive information. To ask for removal of support correspondence, email us at the address above; legal retention requirements may apply.

8. Website hosting

Monease's privacy and support pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the service. Monease does not add its own advertising or behavioral analytics tracking scripts to these static pages. For more information about GitHub's practices, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or object to processing of certain personal data. Contact atilla.poyraz1998@gmail.com with a specific request. You can also manage Apple subscriptions through your Apple Account settings.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect product or legal changes. The last updated date and relevant information will be revised on this page.
